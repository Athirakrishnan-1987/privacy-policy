CodeRabbit Triage

Prioritize PRs by impact, not arrival order.

When anyone on the team can create a PR, your queue descends into chaos. CodeRabbit Triage helps you ship value faster.

Book a demoOpen Triage

PRs can arrive before planning.

PRs increasingly arrive fully realized from diverse systems and agents.

Planning is being bypassed. Software development is no longer a linear progression that starts with the deliberate scoping of work.

Developers proactively create PRs. With the help of AI, developers often jump from idea to implementation.

Background agents create PRs from alerts. Products and monitoring systems can trigger automated fixes, including work that may not be needed or wanted.

The endless PR stream

PRs are piling up faster than teams can sort through and review them.

More PRs overall.

Teams and long-running agents are shipping more and longer PRs.

Teams struggle to triage & review them.

Endless PRs mean teams waste time managing queue logistics and prioritizing reviews.

More PRs become stale.

Agent-written PRs can sit in queues until their context, value, or relevance has changed.

PR triage becomes the new center of gravity.

Triage now handles decisions that once belonged to planning, including whether PRs are important or coherent with other work.

How CodeRabbit Triage works

We give you the visibility you need to order the queue.

  3. 1

    Explains your queue

    A Kanban board shows your PRs and why each one is prioritized.

  4. 2

    Prioritizes value

    Ranks PRs by business impact.

  5. 3

    Ranks risk

    Shows risk signals to guide the depth of review.

  6. 4

    Flags overlap & staleness

    Highlights PRs that overlap or are no longer relevant.

  7. 5

    Suggests reviewers

    Choose a reviewer from the picker and send a review request on GitHub.
